# Programme screening specification

For each candidate city, find formal programmes that enable non-state actors to install, host or maintain micro-scale nature-based solutions. Combine the city and relevant authorities with intervention and support-instrument terms; use local-language equivalents. Inspect official municipal, utility and government-linked documents. Do not use model-generated summaries as evidence.

Require all eight eligibility conditions in `protocol/eligibility.csv`. Retain one-off pilots only when recurrence, renewal, continuing budget or institutional adoption is documented. Record the programme name, structured support, operative sources, access date, screening decision and the reason for exclusion when relevant. Flag renamed programmes, duplicates, wrong-geography records and multi-jurisdiction schemes for identity reconciliation; do not merge distinct offers solely because they share an authority.

The manuscript assigns this screening role to the first GPT-4o custom assistant. The second assistant separately reassesses eligibility and extracts the programme. Researchers control inclusion decisions and adjudication.
